# Quantum-Pump-State-Crossover

**Right photon counts, non-Gaussian joint state.**

A bright coherent pump can become increasingly undepleted in relative energy
while its quantum back-action leaves the generated signal and idler finitely
far from every single Gaussian state. In the same regime, the complete
pair-count distribution and each individual thermal marginal approach the
usual classical-pump predictions.

## Controlled crossover

For the closed resonant three-mode Hamiltonian

$$
H=i\hbar g(ca^\dagger b^\dagger-c^\dagger ab),\qquad
|\Psi(0)\rangle=|\alpha\rangle_c|0,0\rangle_{ab},\qquad N=\alpha^2,
$$

consider the growing gain

$$
r=\alpha gt=\frac12\ln(1+8\alpha\lambda)
$$

at fixed finite $\lambda>0$. An explicit residual quantum state approximates
the exact dynamics with a norm error $O_\lambda(\ln\alpha/\alpha)$. Its
physical-frame description is a two-mode squeezed vacuum with a Gaussian
phase average of variance $1/(4N)$, generated by tracing out the quantum pump
rather than by adding technical noise.

The mean converted fraction satisfies $2\lambda/\sqrt N+o(N^{-1/2})$, while
the pump and joint-output purity approach

$$
\mathcal P(\lambda)=\int_0^\infty e^{-z-\lambda^2z^2}\,dz<1.
$$

A phase-referenced measurement of the rescaled squeezed quadrature has
characteristic function

$$
\varphi_\lambda(\xi)=\frac{e^{-\xi^2/4}}{\sqrt{1+2\lambda^2\xi^2}},
$$

which gives a bounded-measurement separation from every individual Gaussian
state, including mixed or retuned ones. The physical quadrature scale shrinks
as $N^{-1/4}$: this is not a fixed-resolution device claim.

The model, quantum-pump mechanism, Gaussian interaction frames, and historical
high-gain restrictions are established. The result assessed here is the
uniformly controlled nondegenerate crossover and the separation of its
number-statistical and quantum-state validity criteria.

The output approaches a convex mixture of Gaussian states, not a single
Gaussian state. No Wigner-negativity or convex-Gaussian-hull separation is
claimed. Total-number-conserving measurements remain asymptotically accurate;
photon-counting applications are not automatically invalidated. Entanglement
identified by pump impurity is across pump versus both outputs, not a separate
claim about signal versus idler.
